EMBER PACT

A duel of cinders. Play two cards of one suit and its Resonance wakes.

Goal: reduce the enemy hero to 0. You have 30 life; so does Rook.

Play: click a card to play it (or press 1–7). Cards cost embers; your embers refill every turn.

Resonance: two cards of the same suit in one turn trigger a bonus — ▲ Flame burns, ● Tide draws, ◆ Thorn shields, ★ Storm refills embers.

Guard: Guard creatures take your creatures' blows before their hero does.

Keys: 1–7 play a card · Space ends your turn · M mutes.
